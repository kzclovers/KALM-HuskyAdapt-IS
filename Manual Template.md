# Introduction
The Bop and Groove Tiger lights up, speaks, and dances when its buttons are pressed. This guide explains how to adapt the “Party Time” and “Foot” buttons located on the tiger’s feet so they can be activated using an external switch connected through an audio jack. While this manual focuses on adapting these two buttons, additional modifications are possible.

> For more information, reach out to HuskyADAPT at adaptuw@uw.edu.

# Safety Precautions

This adaptation requires the use of a soldering iron and drill. When drilling or soldering, adhere to the following safety guidelines:
- Be alert and aware of your surroundings.
- Wear safety goggles.
- Tie back long hair and secure loose-fitting clothing.
- Take all batteries out of the toy.

When soldering, adhere to the following safety guidelines:
- Ensure that you are in a well-ventilated room.
- Ensure soldering iron cords will not be tripped over.
- Keep your hands away from the iron tip, soldering connection, or anything that has recently touched the tip.
